Orders in the bundled mini-project can now carry an optional coupon code.

- `OrderRequest` accepts an optional `coupon_code`; omitting it means "no coupon", and the existing
  positional construction `OrderRequest(order_id, total)` keeps working.
- The wire payload returned by `create_order` always contains a `couponCode` key: the code, or `null`
  when there is none. The other keys do not change.
- Wire payloads are also read back (the retry queue calls `replay` and uses the serializer's
  `from_json` directly): the coupon must survive that round trip, and payloads recorded before this
  change, which have no `couponCode` key, must still be readable.

Do not modify the existing tests.
