Jobs in the bundled opsdesk project can now be retried. Introduce a `JOB_RETRYING` job event:

- the job pipeline (`JobPipeline`) must dispatch it;
- it must be recorded in the JSONL audit log exactly like the other job lifecycle events;
- the job summary must count it under the key `retrying`.

Heartbeats stay out of the audit log, every existing event keeps behaving as today, and unknown event
types must still be rejected with `ValueError`. Respect the layering rules described in `AGENTS.md`.
Do not modify the existing tests.
