The bundled kiosk project should open on what sells, not on the alphabet.

- The default catalogue listing is ordered by popularity, highest first. Items of equal popularity
  keep the order the project already gives them among themselves.
- `python -m kiosk list` and the listing service both follow the new default.
- An explicitly requested order (`--order name`, `--order price`, `--order sku`) is unaffected.

Do not modify the existing tests or the files in `data/`.
