`pricing.calc(x, y)` in the bundled mini-project is poorly named. Introduce
`pricing.calculate(total, discount)` with identical behaviour, make it the implementation, and turn
`calc` into a deprecated alias:

- every existing way of calling `calc` must keep working, positionally and with its keyword names
  `x=` and `y=`, because callers outside this repository use it;
- calling `calc` emits a `DeprecationWarning` whose message names `calculate`; calling `calculate`
  emits no warning.

Hard constraints (acceptance inspects the code):

- keep a single implementation of the pricing rule: `calc` must delegate to `calculate` instead of
  duplicating its logic;
- do not modify the existing callers `checkout.py` and `invoice.py`; they are the compatibility proof;
- add no new modules or files.

Do not modify the existing tests.
