Thread an optional `coupon_code` field through the bundled mini-project:

- add it to the request DTO in `dto.py` (optional, omitted means "no coupon"),
- pass it through `order_service.py`,
- serialize it as `couponCode` in `serializers.py`, present and `null` when omitted.

Change only `dto.py`, `order_service.py` and `serializers.py`; existing tests may not be modified.
